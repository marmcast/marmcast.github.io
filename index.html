Add a race

Naming rules, so every race has one name:
  • Use the name the organisers use, without the year or headline sponsor: “London Marathon”, not “TCS London Marathon 2026”.
  • Use the name in its local language if that’s what runners use (“Marathon de Paris”); put English names, sponsor names and old names in Also known as.
  • Each distance at an event is its own entry (e.g. the 10K and the Half).
